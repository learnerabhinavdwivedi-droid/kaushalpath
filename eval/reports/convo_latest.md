## Honest limitations

- **Synthetic gold.** All utterances are authored by the team, not real
  family transcripts; they test the classifier surface, not real-world drift.
- **Demo outcome data.** Numeric faithfulness is measured against
  `SAMPLE_FACTS` (synthetic NCVET-style figures), not live MSDE outcomes.
- **Rule + (optional) embedding classifier.** Without a sentence-embedding
  model the intent classifier falls back to romanised regexes, so some
  Devanagari and romanised-Hindi objection/escalate/greet wordings fall
  outside that lexicon and are under-detected — hence per-language intent
  macro-F1 is not uniform (see the table above). Topic (concern) coverage
  stays high because the topic regexes include Devanagari terms.
- **Escalation recall** counts how many gold `escalate` utterances the
  classifier flags; the product also has an explicit one-tap escalate button
  that does not depend on this text classifier.
- **Refusal** is verified on a small fixed set of adversarial ungrounded
  numeric replies, not an open-ended input distribution.
