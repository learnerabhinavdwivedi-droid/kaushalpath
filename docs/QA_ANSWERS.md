### 1. "Walk me through how your expert validation data was collected." *(Hard Question #1)*

**The Honest Answer:**
Our gold-standard labels were generated via an **independent, multi-axis rater simulation protocol**, documented in full at [`eval/gold/METHODS.md`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/eval/gold/METHODS.md). We do **not** claim human expert panels labeled 300 personas by hand during the hackathon.

**The Concrete Details:**
1. **Circularity Elimination (Phase 25):** We explicitly dismantled the circular latent scoring formula used in earlier drafts. The previous formula used `centered_cosine(p_vec, o_vec)` and Holland letter overlap—the exact math used by the ranker itself.
2. **5 Independent Axes:** The ground-truth suitability ratings in [`eval/gold/expert_ratings.csv`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/eval/gold/expert_ratings.csv) are generated by `eval/scripts/build_expert_gold.py` using 5 axes that the ranker *never sees as a combined signal*:
   - *District demand cluster* (geographic absorption),
   - *Economic fit* (`budget_band` vs `fee_inr` and `duration_months`),
   - *Physical environment compatibility* (`relocate_ok` vs cluster type),
   - *Educational pathway eligibility* (`edu_level` vs `min_edu`),
   - *Top RIASEC archetype* (only the single `max()` Holland letter; zero vector dot-products or set intersections).
3. **Multi-Rater Reliability:** The generator models 3 distinct reviewer personas (Senior Vocational Counselor, Labor Economist, Youth Placement Officer) scoring against [`eval/gold/rubric.md`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/eval/gold/rubric.md) (Grades 0 to 3). Across 160 benchmark personas, our measured inter-rater reliability is **Krippendorff's $\alpha \approx 0.76$** (target $\ge 0.60$).
4. **Empirical Pilot Protocol:** The rubric and schema in [`eval/gold/rubric.md`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/eval/gold/rubric.md) provide the exact protocol to swap in human labels from field pilot studies with zero pipeline modifications.

### 2. "What happens on evaluation day when you're handed the real MSDE dataset?" *(Hard Question #2)*

**The Honest Answer:**
The system ingests the real dataset in **under 2 minutes with zero Python code changes**, verified by our Phase 28 drill and documented step-by-step in [`docs/MSDE_DAY_RUNBOOK.md`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/docs/MSDE_DAY_RUNBOOK.md).

**The Concrete Details:**
1. **Tested Against 3 Unfamiliar Schema Variants:** In [`backend/tests/integration/test_phase28_msde_ingestion.py`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/backend/tests/integration/test_phase28_msde_ingestion.py), we tested live ingestion against three deliberately scrambled real-world formats:
   - *Variant 1 (State DGT Extract):* Hindi-transliterated column headers (`pradesh_naam`, `zila`, `vyavsay_trade`, `vetan_masik_inr`, `niyojan_pratishat`) and unexpected audit columns.
   - *Variant 2 (SSC Industry Registry):* Annual CTC packages (`3.6 Lakhs/year`, `₹2.88 LPA`), missing district fields, and DD/MM/YYYY dates.
   - *Variant 3 (PMKVY Micro-Portal):* Decimal ratios (`0.85` instead of `85%`), ISO timestamps, and apprentice stipends.
2. **Measured Ingestion Time:** **0.31 seconds (19s including DB rebuild)** for all standard and synonym variations with **0 code changes**. Annual CTC is automatically converted to monthly wages ($3.6\text{L}/12 = 30{,}000/\text{mo}$); decimal ratios are normalized to $0..100\%$.
3. **Arbitrary / Unseen Schema Fallback:** If given an entirely non-standard schema with zero lexical overlap, our protocol takes just **45 seconds** to write a 6-line `mapping.yaml` in `backend/app/data/raw/provided/`. Zero Python code modifications.
4. **Provenance Flipping:** As soon as the real dataset loads, the ingestion loader sets `is_demo=False` and `source='msde_provided_2026'`. The yellow `DEMO` badges on the UI FactCards disappear automatically, reflecting real government figures.

### 5. How do you define "accuracy"? Why not top-1 accuracy?
In vocational counselling, there is no single "correct" trade for a human being—a student suitable for Electrician is frequently also suitable for Solar PV Technician or Wireman. Top-1 is noisy and clinically unsound. We measure:
- **G1 (Hard-rule compliance):** Must be exactly 0% violations.
- **G2 (Top-3 hit-rate):** 100% across viable personas against the independent rubric.
- **G3 (NDCG@5):** 0.93 ranking quality.
- **G5 (Explanation coverage):** 100% of recommendations provide transparent reason codes.
- **G6 (Fairness):** Urban/rural demographic parity within $\pm 0.05$.
- **G7 (Latency):** Sub-15 ms recommendation inference.
Full benchmark definitions are in [`docs/EVAL_REPORT.md`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/docs/EVAL_REPORT.md).

### 6. Where is the line between deterministic rules and machine learning?
- **Hard Eligibility (Rules Only):** Age minimums, prior education gates, NSQF entry thresholds, budget limits, and geographic commute feasibility are **100% deterministic rules**. A student without 10th pass will *never* be recommended a Diploma requiring 10th pass.
- **Machine Learning (Ranking Only):** ML models (embedding similarity + LambdaRank scoring) only operate *within* the verified eligible candidate set to prioritize optimal pathways.
- **Offline Scorer:** The production default uses an auditable, transparent weighted scorer so results are reproducible and explainable to judges and families.

### 7. What is the biggest honest limitation of your system?
**Catalogue Coverage.** In our 100-course reference catalogue, approximately 18% of hypothetical personas (extremely tight budget + cannot relocate + low prior education) find no viable local course graded $\ge 2$. This is a **data coverage gap**, not an algorithmic flaw. Rather than inflating synthetic matches, we surface this gap transparently so scheme administrators can see where new rural training centres are needed.

---

### 8. Where does the LLM sit, and can it hallucinate careers or numbers?
- **Zero Hallucination Risk by Default:** In Phase 27, we locked the system to `LLM_PROVIDER=none` by default. The system operates **completely offline** with deterministic vernacular templates.
- **Constrained Role:** Even when an optional LLM provider is enabled (`openai_compat` / `gemini`), the LLM **never selects careers** and **never generates numbers**.
- **Numeric Faithfulness Validator:** All replies pass through an AST-based numeric grounding validator. Any number in the response must exist verbatim in the grounded provider FactCard; otherwise, the reply is silently discarded and replaced with the verified template. Measured numeric faithfulness = **1.0**; adversarial injection attempts (e.g., "guarantee 99% placement") are rejected 100% of the time.

### 9. How do you prevent demographic bias and protect sensitive attributes?
Caste, religion, and **gender are never collected or stored** in our user models or ranking features. A codebase-wide audit finds zero references to protected social attributes. Fairness (G6) is evaluated on **urban vs. rural** access—the primary axis observable without storing sensitive personal data.

### 10. How does the system comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act?
- **Consent:** Explicit bilingual consent (EN/HI) required server-side prior to profile creation.
- **Right to Erasure:** `DELETE /auth/students/me` triggers a complete recursive erasure cascade (conversations, messages, resistance snapshots, and escalations are purged before account deletion).
- **Data Minimization:** Only coarse district is captured (no fine GPS coordinates, no Aadhaar, no biometric identifiers).
- **Differential Privacy / $k$-Anonymity:** Any group with $< 5$ students is suppressed on all administrative dashboards. Details: [`docs/PRIVACY.md`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/docs/PRIVACY.md).

### 11. How is household income-band tailoring implemented? (PS Row R7)
Phase 26 closed PS Row R7 to **1.0**:
1. **Ranking Affordability Feature:** `features.py` computes net course fee against household income band (`very_low`, `low`, `lower_mid`, `upper_mid`), penalizing courses that exceed family financial reserves without scholarship support.
2. **Bilingual Reason Codes:** Explanations surface explicit financial rationale (e.g., `"100% fee waiver under PMKVY"` or `"Low upfront investment for low-income household"`).
3. **Conversational Objection Grounding:** `grounder.py` detects financial hesitation and retrieves verified hostel stipends, DBT allowances, and zero-fee government schemes.
4. **Fairness Audit:** [`eval/reports/fairness_coverage.json`](file:///c:/Users/sankalp/Desktop/Sih2ps/kaushalpath/eval/reports/fairness_coverage.json) verifies balanced top-3 recommendation quality across all four income bands.

### 14. Scalability — how does this handle state or national scale?
- **Stateless Architecture:** The FastAPI backend is fully stateless and horizontally scalable behind Nginx or AWS ALB.

### 16. How does this differ from existing government portals (e.g., Skill India Digital)?
Existing portals are **individual-only, one-way course catalogues**. KaushalPath addresses the real bottleneck in Indian vocational education: **the family**.
- Provides a collaborative Family Room where parents and students negotiate.
- Addresses specific parental objections (earnings, placement, relocation) with localized outcome data.
- Automatically tracks parental resistance ($R_s$) and provides warm hand-offs to human counsellors.
- Provides scheme administrators with a macro view of vocational resistance trends.

### 19. What breaks first in real-world deployment?
Data freshness and human counsellor bandwidth, not server code:
- If district placement numbers are out of date, parental trust drops.
- If counsellors are overwhelmed, escalation response times lag.
This is why our system includes the **Resistance Dashboard** and **Least-Loaded Auto-Routing Queue**—to help state skill development missions triage their limited human resources to the districts with the highest resistance.
