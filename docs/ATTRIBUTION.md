### Components Used:
- `Career Interest Types` & `Interests Illustrative Activities`: Per-occupation Holland RIASEC vectors (realistic, investigative, artistic, social, enterprising, conventional).
- `Abilities` & `Knowledge`: Empirical cognitive, spatial, and mechanical ability requirements mapped to vocational occupations.
- `Work Context`: Environmental and physical characteristics (outdoor, hazard, physical effort, customer contact) for family-facing decision support.
- `Job Zones`: Education and preparation level indicators for NSQF sanity-checking.

## 2. Open Government Data (India) & Scheme Portals
- **DGT (Directorate General of Training):** Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS), ITI directory, grading metrics, STRIVE tracer studies.
- **NCVET / NQR:** National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF) qualification packs and course master data.
- **Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE):** PMKVY scheme guidelines and placement outcomes data.
- **National Career Service (NCS) / MoLE:** National Classification of Occupations (NCO-2015).
- **Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI):** Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) aggregates.
